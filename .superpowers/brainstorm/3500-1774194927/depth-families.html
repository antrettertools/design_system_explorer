Depth Families — Every Hue Gets Pale · Vivid · Deep

Tints and shades aren't an afterthought — they're a first-class part of the slot system

New concept: "depth level" per hue. Each hue in the palette (primary or secondary) can appear at three depths: pale (L 0.86–0.93, C low — backgrounds, hovers), vivid (L 0.52–0.72, C high — buttons, highlights), deep (L 0.22–0.38, C mid — text on light, dark surfaces). The slot fill queue picks from depth families intelligently so you never get two slots that look too similar.
