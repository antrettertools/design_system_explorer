A — Structured Role Rows

Each slot (Brand, Secondary, Accent A, Accent B, Typography) is a labeled row with its shade scale visible. Lock per slot. Very clear, role-aware from the start. Vibe coders immediately understand "brand color" vs guessing.

Pros

  • Role labels from day 1
  • Shade scale always visible
  • Easy to scan what's locked

Cons

  • Slightly denser UI
  • Less "magical" feel

B — Coolors-Style Big Swatches

Large immersive color columns, hex visible. Click to lock, space to cycle unlocked. Typography as a compact row below. Role labels only appear after locking. Maximally playful and familiar — closest to coolors.co.

Pros

  • Most playful / fun
  • Proven UX pattern
  • Low cognitive load

Cons

  • Roles hidden until locked
  • Less color science visible

C — Hybrid: Swatches + Instant Scale ★

Big playful color swatches (coolors feel) with role labels baked in from the start. When a color is locked, its shade strip appears below it. Typography is a compact row with scale preview inline. The vibe coder sees both the fun AND the derived system without switching views.

Pros

  • Playful AND informative
  • Shade scale appears as you lock
  • Role labels guide vibe coders
  • Export visible from minute 1

Cons

  • Most complex to build of the three
