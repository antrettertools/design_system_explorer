Senior UX Suggestions
Suggestion SplitPane divider hit area too small
  • Divider is only 4px wide — very hard to grab
  • Add a transparent wider hit zone via ::before/::after pseudo (e.g. 12px)
  • Or show a subtle center grip indicator on hover
Suggestion TypographySpecimen heading clips text
  • white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis
  • Heading specimen text gets silently clipped at certain widths
  • Change to 2-line max with -webkit-line-clamp: 2
Suggestion ShadeStrip label unreadable on light swatches
  • Labels always use rgba(255,255,255,0.55)
  • On very light shade cells this is invisible
  • Mirror the data-light adaptive logic from ColorSlotCard
Suggestion ContrastGrid cells lack hover feedback
  • Cells are informational but don't indicate they're readable
  • A subtle scale or border highlight on hover would improve scannability
Suggestion Sessions drawer close/delete icons need padding
  • Text × and ✕ chars have poor tap targets
  • Lucide X with proper button padding = better accessibility
  • Also: add transition: background var(--ui-duration-2) to closeBtn
